# OZ-TAL 2026

## One-line thesis

Use off-the-shelf VLM representations for online zero-shot temporal action localization of unseen action categories.

## Relevance to This Project

This is the major threat if the project drifts toward VLM, unseen-action, open-vocabulary, or zero-shot online TAL claims.

## Overlap

- online temporal action localization;
- VLM features;
- unseen/open-vocabulary classes;
- memory/state-machine style online span prediction.

## Key Difference We Need

Stay closed-set and trainable unless directly benchmarking against OZ-TAL. Focus on causal event-state hypothesis tracking, revision/commit protocol, and task-aligned online training.

## Blocked Claims

- first VLM route for online TAL;
- online zero-shot TAL novelty;
- open-vocabulary streaming action localization novelty without primary comparison.
