# StreamReady 2026

## One-line thesis

Streaming video understanding should jointly optimize answer correctness and readiness, penalizing both speculative early responses and delayed responses.

## Method

The framework learns a readiness mechanism and evaluates it with an Answer Readiness Score tied to annotated evidence windows.

## Relevance to This Project

StreamReady makes a generic "learn when enough evidence has arrived" claim non-novel. A CESR commit mechanism must be specific to temporal action instances, risk-set stopping, tIoU/class quality, and GT-end latency.
