# Online Anomaly Detection in Surveillance Videos with Asymptotic Bounds on False Alarm Rate

## One-line thesis

Performs online video anomaly detection while deriving an asymptotic control on false-alarm rate.

## Problem / Gap

Video anomaly detectors deployed continuously must account for false alarms, not only finite-clip discrimination.

## Method

Uses an online anomaly-scoring and decision procedure with an asymptotic false-alarm analysis.

## Key Results

Establishes that video-domain sequential false-alarm control predates the proposed semantic-event alarm route.

## Assumptions

The guarantee is asymptotic and tied to the anomaly-detection setup.

## Limitations / Failure Modes

Anomaly detection does not provide closed/open-vocabulary semantic event identity, repeated-instance handling, or temporal-span localization.

## Reusable Ingredients

Long-stream video evaluation and false-alarm-rate baselines.

## Open Questions

What semantic-event-specific scientific gap remains after matching this operational risk framing?
