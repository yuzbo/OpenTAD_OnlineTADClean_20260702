# Blocked Main Claim: Open-Vocabulary / Zero-Shot Online TAL

## Blocked Thesis

Use VLMs or language prompts to claim online zero-shot or unseen-action temporal localization novelty.

## Why Blocked

OZ-TAL 2026 already covers Online Zero-Shot TAL with off-the-shelf VLM, unseen/open-vocabulary action classes, memory/enhancement, background-aware classification, and strict online span prediction.

## Allowed Scope

Closed-set CESR/PCEH can use frozen visual representations, but must not drift into zero-shot/open-vocabulary claims unless OZ-TAL is a primary baseline and the delta is explicit.
