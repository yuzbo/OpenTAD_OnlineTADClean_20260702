# EventMATR D1 novelty verdict

Status: **conditionally promising; not yet paper-proven**.

## Allowed wording now

EventMATR D1 investigates a unified strict-causal On-TAL event process that
couples stable pre-birth assignment, censored birth/end risks, explicit
post-birth ownership, cancellation/reacquisition, and event-level
train/inference unroll alignment.

## Forbidden wording now

- first use of survival hazards;
- first identity-preserving query model;
- first scheduled-sampling or autoregressive online training;
- first overlapping or same-class On-TAL detector;
- paper-valid superiority before locked-test, multi-seed, and registered
  ablations are legitimately released.

## Novelty gate

The composite claim survives only if isolated experiments show that C1–C4
repair distinct, measured lifecycle failures and the full TH combination
improves strict-causal detection and lifecycle quality beyond strong baselines.
Until then, every component and the composite remain hypotheses.
